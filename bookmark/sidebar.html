Settings

Display Mode (Sidebar)
When on, clicking the icon opens the sidebar; when off, it opens the popup.
Search
Show search box to quickly find bookmarks.
Short Names
Show only the site name, like youtube, instead of the full page title.
Page Previews
Hover a card to see the site. Opening a bookmarked site saves a small screenshot on this device. Until then, the card shows the site's share image, downloaded once without cookies.
Hover Tooltips
Show full bookmark titles on hover.
Open in New Tab
Open bookmarks in a new tab vs the current one.
Keep Panel Open
Open in background tabs to keep the panel visible.
